## Chapter: Cold Open

Seventeen days, that's all that's left before GTA6 is in your hands. Not a rumor, not a leak, not a "trust me bro" — seventeen real days. So today we're wiping the slate clean with the full status check: everything Rockstar and Take-Two have actually confirmed, no speculation, no leaked garbage, just facts, right up to today.

## Chapter: Release Date

Let's start with the date that matters more than any other: GTA6 launches November 19th, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and Series S. That date hasn't moved since Rockstar locked it in, and there's still no PC version announced, so if you're on PC, you're waiting on a later port, full stop. No official window's been given for that, so anyone telling you a PC date right now is guessing. Console only, November 19th — lock that in.

## Chapter: Price And Editions

Now the part that's been blowing up timelines for months, price. Confirmed pricing is $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate, a jump from what GTA5 launched at, and the internet's had a lot of opinions — we've covered the backlash in a separate video, but here's what you're paying for. Standard gets you the base game, Ultimate adds bonus in-game content. There's also a physical edition, but here's the catch: the retail copy is a code in a box, not a disc, so you're still downloading the whole game. If you were hoping to skip the download by buying physical, that's not how this one works. And if you buy Standard now and change your mind, Rockstar's confirmed you can buy the Ultimate upgrade separately at any time — you're not locked in forever.

## Chapter: Platforms And Special Hardware

On platforms, again, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only at launch. No last-gen version, no Series S corner-cutting detailed beyond standard resolution scaling, and no PC. Sony has also confirmed limited-edition hardware tied to launch — two GTA 6 DualSense controllers at $84.99 each, PS5 only, shipping the same day as the game. If you're a collector, that's confirmed and real, not a mockup.

## Chapter: Protagonists

Who are you actually playing? Confirmed since the first trailer and unchanged since — two protagonists, Lucia and Jason. A Bonnie-and-Clyde dynamic sits at the center of the story, and every piece of official marketing has kept both of them front and center. This isn't a "reveal later" situation, it's been locked from day one, and it's the first time the series has put two playable leads at the core of the main story.

## Chapter: Setting And Map

The setting: Vice City and the surrounding Leonida region, Rockstar's fictional take on Florida, confirmed straight from the marketing, not a leak. In practice that means swampland, small towns, and a sprawling coastal city connected in one map, a scale Rockstar's called their biggest yet. We're not going to give you leaked map coordinates or dimensions pulled from a data-mine — that's unverified, and airing that kind of content is exactly what gets channels in trouble. What's confirmed is confirmed: Vice City, Leonida, bigger than anything they've built before.

## Chapter: Multiplayer And Monetization

Here's one that matters if you're planning your first week: there's no multiplayer mode confirmed for launch, and Take-Two's CEO said in September there's no recurrent spending planned for launch. This is a single-player-first release. That doesn't mean GTA Online's successor never happens, it almost certainly does down the line, but on day one this is about Jason and Lucia's story, not a live-service grind. If your plan was jumping into online night one, that plan needs to change.

## Chapter: Pre-Orders And Pre-Load Status

Pre-orders have been live since late June 2026, so if you haven't locked yours in, that window's already been open for months — you're not early, you're just catching up. And pre-loading, downloading the game ahead of launch so it's ready the second the clock hits midnight, is confirmed to open November 12th, 2026, exactly one week before launch. We've got a full video coming closer to that date walking you through how to set that up, so if storage and pre-load logistics are your concern, that one's for you.

## Chapter: Where Things Stand

So here's where we actually are, seventeen days out. Date's locked, November 19th. Price is locked, $80 Standard, $100 Ultimate. Platforms are locked, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, no PC yet. Protagonists are locked, Lucia and Jason. Setting is locked, Vice City and Leonida. No multiplayer, no recurrent spending at launch. Pre-orders are open, pre-load opens November 12th. That's not hype, that's not a leak, that's just where GTA6 actually stands today, straight from Rockstar and Take-Two.
