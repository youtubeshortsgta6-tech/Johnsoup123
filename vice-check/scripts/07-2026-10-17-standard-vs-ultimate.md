## Chapter: Cold Open

Twenty extra dollars — that's the gap between GTA6's Standard Edition and its Ultimate Edition. But almost nobody online is actually listing out what that twenty dollars buys, they're just arguing about whether it's worth it. So today, we're doing the thing nobody else did: every vehicle, weapon, business, and cosmetic, confirmed, item by item, straight from Rockstar's own announcement. By the end you'll know exactly what you're paying for.

## Chapter: The Base Numbers

Standard Edition is seventy-nine ninety-nine, Ultimate is ninety-nine ninety-nine, both confirmed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S, with pre-orders opening June 25th and a PC version expected to follow console launch. Preloading opens November 12th, a full week ahead of the November 19th release date — and for physical copies, you get a download code in the box, not a disc, so preloading is built into how Rockstar wants everyone playing at midnight regardless of format.

## Chapter: What Standard Edition Gets You

Standard Edition is the full base game — the entire story campaign with both protagonists, Jason and Lucia, the complete open world, and everything Rockstar has shown in the trailers and reveal materials. On top of that, anyone who pre-orders, regardless of edition, gets the Vintage Vice City Pack at no extra cost — retro outfits and hairstyles for both protagonists, a 1955 Vapid Stanier with its own garage, and a tropical weapon pattern nodding to the original Vice City. Digital pre-orders also get a free month of GTA Plus. So even at the eighty-dollar tier, pre-ordering gets you the full story plus a themed bonus pack, not a stripped-down product.

## Chapter: What Ultimate Edition Adds

Here's where the extra twenty dollars goes. The Ultimate Edition layers in five additional vehicles — a 1995 Grotti Cheetah, a 1967 Vapid Dominator Buggy, a Dinka Enduro Motorcycle, a Shitzu Squalo Boat, and a Crest Kayak — plus exclusive firearms: a matching his-and-hers pair of Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers, a personalized ES9 pistol for Jason, and a personalized Klose K17 for Lucia. For cosmetics, there's the Vice City Style Pack, with exclusive outfits, tattoos, and cosmetic items, plus a separate Goodtime Gear Pack.

## Chapter: The Business And Property Bonuses

This is the part most price breakdowns skip entirely: Ultimate Edition also unlocks six in-game businesses and locations right out of the gate — Rideout Customs, One-Eyed Willie's mod shop, Sara's Unisex Salon, the Stock 305 clothing store, Electric Fang Tattoo, and Paradise Garage, a weapon locker and fence — plus the Ganado Retro Build mod kit, a customization set for modifying vehicles. So this isn't just cosmetic bonus content, it's functional access to shops and services that tie into gameplay from day one instead of having to unlock or find them organically.

## Chapter: The Exclusive Story Extras

Ultimate Edition also includes two story extras you won't get on Standard — access to the PTT Youngin$ illegal goods store, and a Classic Car Collection commission from a character named Wyman. Rockstar's own language described the Ultimate bonuses as "woven throughout Jason and Lucia's story," suggesting they're built to feel like part of the narrative rather than bolted-on DLC.

## Chapter: The One Detail That Changes The Math

Here's the detail that matters most if you're on the fence — Rockstar has confirmed you can buy the Ultimate upgrade separately at any time. The upgrade price hasn't been published yet. That changes the calculus: you don't have to commit eighty versus a hundred dollars blind on day one, you can buy Standard, play the story, decide if you want the extra vehicles, weapons, businesses, and extras, and upgrade later once you know — a meaningfully different situation than past "definitive edition" scenarios that locked you into your choice at purchase.

## Chapter: So What Are You Actually Paying For

So let's tally it up. For eighty dollars with a pre-order, you get the complete story, the full open world, and the Vintage Vice City Pack. For an extra twenty, you're adding five vehicles, four weapons, two cosmetic packs, six businesses, a vehicle mod kit, and two story extras — plus the option to decide later instead of now. Whether that's "worth it" depends on how much you value cosmetic and quality-of-life content versus just wanting the story fast, but now you're deciding with the actual list in front of you, not a headline number.
