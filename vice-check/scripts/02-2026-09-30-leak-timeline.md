## Chapter: Cold Open

He's out of the hospital, he's awaiting retrial, and he's already told reporters he plans to hack again. That's the man behind the biggest leak in gaming history — the one that put ninety unfinished clips of GTA6 online three years before the game even had a release date. This is the full timeline: what leaked, what Rockstar confirmed, and how we got to a locked November 19th release date. No speculation — every date here is checked against real reporting.

## Chapter: The Leak That Broke The Internet

September 2022 — a hacker breaks into Rockstar's internal Slack and pulls out over ninety early development clips and source code fragments from a game Rockstar hadn't even confirmed existed, spreading everywhere within hours. Here's the part most timelines skip — Rockstar didn't deny it; within a day they confirmed the footage was real, unfinished development material, and said it wouldn't change their plans. That single move, confirming instead of denying, is why this channel treats leak legitimacy as its own category. When Rockstar says something's real, it's real; when they don't, we wait.

## Chapter: The Hacker's Fate

The hacker was seventeen, tied to the same group behind the Uber and Nvidia breaches. UK courts found him responsible in 2023 and handed him an indefinite hospital order instead of prison, partly because the court found his autism made him unfit to stand trial. That order didn't stick — as of this year, he's been moved out of the hospital and into custody ahead of a retrial set for November 2026, the same month GTA6 launches, and he's said he plans to hack again. Whether that means a new leak before launch is anyone's guess, but it's exactly why "is this leak real" is still the most-asked question in this fanbase, three years later.

## Chapter: Trailer 1, The First Real Confirmation

December 2023 — Trailer 1 drops early, after someone leaks a low-quality version a day ahead of schedule, forcing Rockstar to rush the real thing out. This is the trailer that confirmed what the 2022 leak only hinted at: a Vice-City-inspired setting called Leonida, and two playable leads, Jason and Lucia, the first female lead of the modern GTA era. It broke YouTube's 24-hour view record for a non-music video — this is the moment the leak officially became the game.

## Chapter: The Delays

From there, the release date moved twice: what was originally eyed for 2025 slipped to May 2026, then slipped again to where it sits now, November 19, 2026. Rockstar's messaging around both delays was the same — they'd rather ship it right than ship it on a date. Say what you want about the wait, but when GTA 5 alone has sold over 230 million copies, you don't need to rush.

## Chapter: Trailer 2, Confirmation At Scale

May 2025 — Trailer 2 lands, and this is where the map, the tone, and the scale of Leonida finally got shown off: swamps, strip malls, a skyline built off real Miami geography. This is also the trailer a fan-made "Trailer 3" tried to piggyback on months later, presenting AI-generated footage as if it were official. It wasn't, and that's exactly why this channel never treats fan-made content as a real leak.

## Chapter: The Price Reveal

Then the number everyone was bracing for: eighty dollars for the Standard Edition, a hundred for Ultimate, which bundles in a Vintage Vice City content pack. It's the first AAA price jump of this console generation, confirmed straight from Rockstar's own pre-order page, not a leak, not a rumor.

## Chapter: The Gameplay Showcase

And this year, the big one: a 26-minute gameplay showcase, the first time the public saw Leonida played rather than shown in a trailer, pulling in over 13 million views on its own. This is the footage this channel treats as ground truth — not because Rockstar says so, but because it's the only long-form gameplay material that's official.

## Chapter: Where We Are Now

So that's the full arc — a hack nobody asked for in 2022, to a confirmed release date in 2026, with every major beat in between confirmed by Rockstar itself, not leaks, not leaker claims, actual statements and actual marketing. November 19th is the date. Everything up to now has been the buildup.
