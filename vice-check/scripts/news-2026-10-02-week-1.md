## Chapter: Story Two: Miami Becomes Vice City

[VISUAL: official Vice City skyline stills; Trailer 2 arena exterior. Do not use the real-world sign photos unless licensed; describe it.]

Story two. On September 22nd, a helicopter lifted a giant pink-and-white "Welcome to Vice City" sign onto the roof of the Kaseya Center in downtown Miami. Rockstar paid Miami-Dade County, which owns the arena, about nine hundred seventy-five thousand dollars for the placement. [VERIFY amount and date.] If that building sounds familiar, it should: it's the real-world match for the Sahara Arena in our Florida video.

Then the bigger deal. County officials approved an arrangement, reported at around three million dollars, that lets Rockstar dress downtown Miami and Miami Beach in Vice City banners, beach chairs and umbrellas starting October 15th, inside a defined downtown zone. [VERIFY amount, start date and zone.] It didn't go through quietly. Earlier in September a county commissioner and the sheriff publicly objected to an idea floated in the same talks: temporarily rebranding Miami International Airport as Vice City Airport. That one isn't happening. The downtown campaign is.

Rating: confirmed, because the money and the permissions come from a government body, not a rumor account. What it means: Rockstar is spending real money on a city that officially isn't in the game. That's the loudest confirmation yet of what Leonida is.

## Chapter: Why Miami Matters for the Map

[VISUAL: Florida video's compare panels: Sahara Arena vs the real arena; Ocean Beach vs South Beach. Then the Leonida outline graphic with Vice City highlighted.]

Here's the part of the Miami story most coverage skipped. Look at where the campaign is allowed: a defined downtown zone between the river and the bay, plus Miami Beach. That's the same slice of the real city that Rockstar's named districts map onto. Ocean Beach is South Beach. The port that Rockstar calls the cruise capital is PortMiami, right on that bay. The arena wearing the sign is the building the Sahara Arena is drawn from. Rockstar didn't buy billboards across Florida. It bought the exact blocks that are in the game.

So when the street campaign starts on October 15th, treat the photos as a free tour of Vice City's downtown before the game ships. Every banner location is a place you'll be able to stand in on November 19th. We'll match them up when the pictures come in. And it settles one more argument for the map video coming October 14th: the city's layout follows real Miami, mainland downtown, a causeway, a beach on the barrier island. Anyone drawing it differently is guessing.

## Chapter: Story Three: What Pre-Orders Actually Get

[VISUAL: Vintage Vice City Pack official art from the press kit; Ultimate Edition stills of the shops and vehicles.]

Story three, and the one people keep asking about. The pre-order bonus is the Vintage Vice City Pack, and here's exactly what's in it. A 1955 Vapid Stanier sedan, with its own garage next to Ocean Beach. Vintage outfits and hairstyles for both Jason and Lucia. And a weapon pattern that puts Tommy Vercetti's palm-tree shirt on most guns. It comes with every digital purchase made by November 20th, and with physical pre-orders while stock lasts. So it's a pre-order bonus that you also get if you buy digitally on launch day. [VERIFY the November 20 cutoff on rockstargames.com/VI/editions.]

Digital pre-orders on the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store also include one free month of GTA+. That's a GTA Online perk: a cash deposit and discounts in the current online game. It has nothing to do with GTA 6 at launch, which has no online mode. Treat it as a coupon, not a feature.

And the Ultimate Edition detail that was easy to miss: its exclusive shops, garages and vendors don't all appear at the start. They unlock chapter by chapter through the story. So the twenty-dollar difference isn't a day-one pile of stuff; it's spread across the game. Rating: confirmed, from Rockstar's own editions page. And the rule from our editions video still holds: Standard first, upgrade later if you want it.
